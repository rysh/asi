### 1. The Genius Problem and IQ

**1-1. The Mystification of Genius**
- IQ rankings have mystified genius
- Von Neumann ~190, Ramanujan ~185+ are extrapolations from biographical anecdote, not measured values
- Numbers that were never measured and cannot exist on the scale are assigned without evidence and take on a life of their own
- *Footnote: Processing Speed Contamination (doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19105953), Who Are We Testing? (doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17745229)*

**1-2. Genius as Outlier**
- Genius has been elevated to authority, made taboo, and mystified
- Or excluded from research to protect the certainty of established findings
- Treated as an outlier that is not a research subject in the first place
- Von Neumann: could not analyze quantum phenomena without macroscopic analogies. Describable as constraint conditions when observed as-is
- Ramanujan: abductive hypothesis generation + brute-force computation. Not mystery but a describable process
- Leibniz: systematic formalization of existing infinitesimal methods
- Aristotle: categorical organization of observational data across domains

**1-3. A Goal That Does Not Exist**
- Claiming to "surpass" something that has not been analyzed is a trivialization of reality
- "Surpassing the best human minds" has no baseline — it is undefinable
- Human cognitive ability has no fixed upper bound
- We are chasing a goal that does not exist

### 2. Critique of Existing Definitions

**2-1. Bostrom: Already Achieved Conditions and a Metaphor**
- Three categories: Speed / Collective / Quality
- Speed and Collective are already achieved (though Bostrom's definition conflates processing throughput with cognitive capability range. Isolate speed as pure throughput and it is already achieved)
- Quality is a metaphor ("the kind of superiority humans have over animals"), not a definition
- "Greatly exceeds human performance in virtually all domains" has no boundary conditions

**2-2. Legg & Hutter: A Definition That Cannot Be Computed**
- Expected reward across all computable environments, weighted by algorithmic complexity
- Incomputable by the authors' own admission
- Ignores resources. A definition that cannot be computed is not a definition

**2-3. Chollet: Calling Infant Learning Intelligence**
- Skill-acquisition efficiency = intelligence
- ARC benchmark is restricted to Spelke's Core Knowledge (the cognitive endowments of human infants)
- Equates infant-level pattern extraction with intelligence as such
- The definition was reverse-engineered to match the benchmark

**2-4. Goertzel: AGI Does Not Scale Into ASI**
- A metaphysical discussion modeled on human intelligence (pre-LLM, 2007)
- Treats AGI → self-improvement → ASI as a continuum
- Treats capability as a scalar value — assumes ASI lies at the far end of quantitative improvement
- AGI and ASI are qualitatively different: reframing (adaptation) vs. abduction (generation)
- Acknowledges embodied learning and the symbol grounding problem, which deserves credit. But does not resolve it
- *Footnote: Intention Without Causation (doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19234682), The Conservation Demarcation (doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21000863)*

### 3. Four Conditions and Discussion

**3-1. Calibrating the Definition**
- The human reference frame is not abandoned ("super" is a relational predicate)
- But "surpass the best of humanity" is undefinable and therefore unreachable
- Move the boundary conditions from the human upper limit to the current capability limits of AI
- Because Speed and Collective are already resolved, the remaining gaps become specifiable
- This decomposition was impossible in 2014. It is possible now, in 2026

**3-2. The Four Conditions**
- Speed ✓ — Processing speed exceeds human cognition by orders of magnitude
- Collective Intelligence ✓ — Cross-domain knowledge integration is already achieved
- Scientific Creativity ✗ — Cannot generate or validate novel hypotheses
- General Wisdom under Complexity ✗ — Judgment breaks down in complex situations

**3-3. No Abduction, No Creativity**
- Solving existing unsolved problems = deduction. Valuable, but not creativity
- Extracting patterns from large datasets = induction. Valuable, but not creativity
- Creativity = posing a question nobody has asked, from an observation nobody has framed
- Abduction (Peirce): the only inference that introduces a new idea
- *Footnote: Lost Abduction and Epistemic Barriers: Toward a Completion Model of Science Communication (doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20005937)*

**3-4. Scaling Is Dead**
- Scaling cannot achieve Condition 3 or Condition 4
- Parameter scaling hit a ceiling → inference-time scaling → chain-of-thought → increased reasoning steps per request
- Workflows, agents, tool calls — things users previously implemented via API — are now wrapped into the model's outer shell
- All mixed together, details undisclosed, benchmarks reported as improved
- Why this fails is explored later

**3-5. Two Challenges, Solved Separately**
- *[Insert image: Distinct Capability Axes — two independent axes (Scientific novelty generation / Metacognitive adaptability), Current AI → Reframing threshold → independent scaling (uploaded file F08FC9CF)]*
- Not connected by a straight line of scaling
- Each requires its own design
- After realization, each must be intentionally designed and improved as a separate capability or it will not grow
- Computing reduces to four logical operations, but without CPU instruction set design and hardware-software coordination, today's performance would not exist. Individual challenges solved by individual design — that is what produced the performance we have

### 4. Defining ASI

**4-1. Speed and Accuracy Are Only Prerequisites**
- Computers are already fast enough
- Not making mistakes is also not a condition for ASI
- AlphaFold, GraphCast, medical imaging, autonomous perception, genome analysis — all orders of magnitude faster and more accurate than humans. But none produce new knowledge
- Speed and accuracy are useful in practice but are not boundary conditions for ASI

**4-2. ASI = Continuous Novel Discovery**
- *[Insert image: Simple triangle loop — Reality / Novel Discovery / Updated Knowledge with "beyond the current frontier" arrow (uploaded file 4491494F)]*
- ASI = the ability to continuously make novel discoveries and inventions, update knowledge, and surpass humans

### 5. What Is AGI?

**5-1. General Does Not Mean Universal**
- General intelligence = adapting to situations the system has not encountered before
- Universal = capable of every possible task. That is not what "general" means
- Performing learned tasks without error is not AGI. That is execution, not adaptation

**5-2. What Does General Mean?**
- The ability to change frames when the current frame does not work
- This is the substance of Condition 3 (Wisdom under Complexity)
- This may sound simple — but implementing it fundamentally means losing controllability
- *Footnote: The Control Impossibility (doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19943538)*

**5-3. The Distinction Between AGI and ASI**
- AGI is the adaptive application of existing knowledge (reframing)
- ASI is the generation of knowledge that does not yet exist (abduction)
- Rearranging and generating are different
- Details in the paper

### 6. The Path to Scientific Creativity

**6-1. LLMs Cannot Judge the Truth of Their Own Discoveries**
- A novel output does not exist in the training data
- LLM fact-checks it → inconsistent with existing data → judged false
- New ideas are killed by the system's own verification

**6-2. The Error of "In the Beginning Was the Word"**
- "Truth is hidden in the world and can be discovered by the right method" = Hermeticism
- Supposedly refuted by the scientific revolution, yet it resurfaces again and again (dormant pathogen)
- Latest symptoms: "scaling will reach true intelligence", "LLMs have a world model"
- Language is a compression of the world, not the world itself
- LLMs can only operate inside the compression
- *Footnote: Intention Without Causation (doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19234682), The Conservation Demarcation (doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21000863)*

**6-3. What Advances Intelligence: The Generation of New Meaning**
- Gödel's incompleteness theorem: a system generates, through self-reference, truths it cannot prove internally
- Standard philosophical interpretation: infinite regression (the same incompleteness repeating)
- My interpretation: infinite progression (generating new meaning to resolve contradiction is not regression but progress through meaning-creation)
- Mathematics has been doing exactly this throughout its history
- Meaning has no conservation law — new dimensions of meaning can be generated without subtracting from any total. This is why infinite progression is structurally possible
- *Footnote: Generative Contradiction: Reading Gödel with Nishida (doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21612954), The Conservation Demarcation (doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21000863)*

**6-4. Is Truth Found or Made?**
- Freud's Thanatos: theoretically dead. No neuroscientific basis. Judged "false" by every accepted method of verification
- Mishima Yukio's seppuku (1970): fused Eros and Thanatos in a single irreversible bodily act
- A theoretically dead concept changed its ontological status through action — an "undead concept"
- Is truth out there in the world, waiting for humans to discover it? Or is truth constituted through action?
- For those who believe the former, this is a deeply uncomfortable joke
- If the former is correct, scaling can reach ASI. If the latter, it is entirely impossible
- *Footnote: Mishima and Thanatos (doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20489614)*

**6-5. Going Outside Language**
- Meaning can be generated (cognitive side, no conservation law)
- But facts on the world side, governed by conservation laws, cannot be generated at will. Observation is the only access
- Meaning generation alone cannot distinguish itself from hallucination
- Verification on the physical side is required: experiment, observation, measurement
- Scientific creativity is achieved only when both the cognitive and physical layers are in place
